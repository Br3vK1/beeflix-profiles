How this works

  • Existing unlock records and equipped titles are preserved when definitions are renamed or disabled.
  • Disabled achievements stop awarding new unlocks and are hidden from the current achievement list; historical unlock records remain stored.
  • Changing a target can make a member eligible on the next achievement refresh. Existing unlocks are never revoked.
  • New definitions currently support movie counts and completed-series counts. Review, request, and community metrics can be added as those systems are implemented.
